Your service is our duty.

Transparent, speedy and time-bound public services are central to the Maharashtra Right to Public Services framework.

MAHARASHTRA RTS ACT, 2015

A simple pathway to understand time-bound public services.

The Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015 came into force on 28 April 2015 to support transparent, prompt and time-bound delivery of notified public services.
